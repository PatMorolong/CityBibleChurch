Steps to Membership

We are thrilled you're considering becoming a member of City Bible Church! Membership is a commitment to connect with our church family and partner with us in our mission. Here are the steps to join:

1

Express Interest

Fill out the interest form below or speak to one of our pastors or leaders after a service. We'd love to connect with you.

2

Attend Orientation

Join our New Members' Orientation class where you'll learn more about our beliefs, vision, and how to get involved.

3

Baptism (If Applicable)

If you haven't been baptized by immersion after salvation, we encourage you to take this important step of faith.

4

Join a Department/Group

Connect with a small group or ministry department to serve, grow, and build relationships within the church family.

5

Welcome!

Once you've completed the steps, you'll be formally welcomed into membership during one of our Sunday services.

Ready to Start?

Fill out this form to let us know you're interested, and we'll contact you with details about the next orientation class.
